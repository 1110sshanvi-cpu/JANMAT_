🏛️
ARTICLE 79 • CONSTITUTION OF INDIA

India's Parliament

Parliament is the Union's central legislative institution.

HOUSE OF THE PEOPLE

Lok Sabha

Members are directly elected by citizens in general elections. It represents people across India's constituencies.

COUNCIL OF STATES

Rajya Sabha

It represents the States and Union Territories. Most members are elected indirectly; some are nominated by the President.

What does Parliament do?

  • Makes laws: considers and debates Bills under the relevant procedure.
  • Discusses national issues: provides a forum for public concerns and policy debate.
  • Checks the government: members ask questions and scrutinise government actions.
  • Considers public finances: Parliament examines taxation and government expenditure.

PARLIAMENTARY CALENDAR

Three commonly known sessions

Parliament meets in sessions; exact dates and schedules can change each year.

FEB–MAY
USUAL PERIOD

Budget Session

Centres on the Union Budget and financial business, alongside legislative work and discussions.

JUL–AUG
USUAL PERIOD

Monsoon Session

Members debate current issues, consider Bills and conduct other parliamentary business.

NOV–DEC
USUAL PERIOD

Winter Session

Provides another period for debates, legislation, questions and scrutiny of government work.

Constitutional rule: The Constitution says six months must not intervene between the last sitting of one session and the first sitting of the next. These months are typical, not guaranteed dates.

Does passing this vote make it a law?

Not by itself. For an ordinary Bill, the real process generally requires passage by both the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha, followed by Presidential assent. Special rules apply to some types of Bills.
